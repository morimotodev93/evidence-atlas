# Research Idea Backlog

These topics are exploratory ideas, not validated research conclusions. No research,
Source collection, or Findings have been completed for this backlog. Evaluation
approaches below are possibilities, not implementation commitments or deadlines.

## 1. National and Regional Trends in LLM Development

Explore differences and variation within LLM development ecosystems in the United
States, China, Europe, Japan, and South Korea.

**Potential research questions:**

- How do model release strategies differ across and within these ecosystems?
- What roles do open-weight and proprietary models play?
- How do language support, deployment preferences, and infrastructure priorities differ?

**Possible evaluation approach:** Compare a diverse, dated sample of model release
documentation and deployment options using consistent categories. Account for
variation within regions; do not infer model behavior from country of origin.

**Status:** Idea — Not Researched

## 2. Cultural Values and Behavioral Differences in LLMs

Explore whether response language, cultural context, training, and alignment
influence model responses, without equating developer location with measured behavior.

**Potential research questions:**

- Does response language influence value judgments?
- Do models exhibit systematic cultural preferences, and how does explicit cultural context affect answers?
- How can observed differences be separated from stereotypes, translation effects, or evaluation artifacts?

**Possible evaluation approach:** Compare matched multilingual prompts with and
without explicit cultural context, repeat responses, and use reviewers from
different backgrounds. Treat training and alignment explanations as hypotheses
unless evidence supports attributing an observed difference to them.

**Status:** Idea — Not Researched

## 3. Creative Writing and Narrative Homogenization in LLMs

Explore the hypothesis that generated fiction converges on similar narrative
structures, moral lessons, or character development.

**Potential research questions:**

- Do models introduce character growth when it is unnecessary or unrequested?
- Do they portray solitude as a problem requiring resolution?
- Do unconventional characters become more socially conventional?
- Do models introduce unnecessary conflict or emotional reconciliation?
- Can models sustain uneventful stories without forcing narrative progression?

**Possible evaluation approach:** Compare repeated outputs from matched prompts
across models, including prompts requesting uneventful or unresolved stories.
Use blinded annotation of narrative patterns and instruction adherence; keep
subjective quality judgments separate from the hypothesized patterns.

**Status:** Idea — Not Researched

## 4. What Do LLM Benchmarks Actually Measure?

Compare the purposes and limitations of human-preference rankings, reasoning
benchmarks, coding evaluations, and creative-writing assessments.

**Potential research questions:**

- How does human preference differ from factual correctness?
- What does Arena measure, and what falls outside its scope?
- How do benchmark design and prompt selection influence rankings?
- Can strong benchmark performance predict real-world application quality?
- How should evaluation results be interpreted across different tasks?

**Possible evaluation approach:** Examine published evaluation protocols, task
selection, scoring rules, and limitations, then compare them with a small set of
application-specific tasks. Separate preference, correctness, and task success;
do not assume that rankings transfer across uses.

**Status:** Idea — Not Researched
